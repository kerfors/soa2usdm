# Re-extraction Acceptance Checklist — usdm_data, sweep 2 (Opus 5.5, prompt v3.8.x)

Criteria the re-extracted corpus is judged against. Written **before** any re-extraction, so a
delta can be classified rather than rationalised. Every item is derived from work already done:
the 22 uncertainty reports of the accepted corpus, the 29 live corrections, the row audit, and the
QC decisions recorded across earlier sessions.

The accepted corpus is the output of sweep 1 (2026-08-17: Opus 5, prompts v3.7.2 and v3.7.3),
promoted in collections `2c6d07c`. Sweep 2 changes model and prompt together, so a delta is
attributed to the two jointly — the sweep does not claim what either contributed on its own.

**How to use it.** A fresh extraction is not accepted because it looks good. It is accepted when
every difference from the baseline below falls into one of three buckets:

| Bucket | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Expected delta** | A v3.8.x rule mandates the new behaviour (§2.1) | Confirm the rule actually applies, then accept |
| **Regression** | Baseline established something the new output loses or contradicts | Do not promote; investigate |
| **New finding** | Neither the baseline nor v3.8.x predicted it | Human review before promotion |

Anything that cannot be placed in a bucket blocks promotion of that table.

A drop in `marks` is the single most serious signal — marks are the data. A drop in `acts` means a
row was lost. A drop in `bind` with `acts` unchanged means notes have come loose from their rows.
Increases are not automatically good: `marks` rising needs a page.

Two tables of the sweep-1 corpus no longer exist and must not reappear: CDISC_Pilot T2 and
NCT04004988 T2 were page-split continuations of their T1 and were merged into it in sweep 1
(CDISC_Pilot T1 carries 71 + 68 = 139 marks). The corpus has no `continuation` and no `reference`
table.

### 2.1 Expected v3.8.x deltas

Mandated by the prompt, so they are accepted once confirmed, not investigated:

- **`review_items` on every table**, and a `## Decisions needed (N)` block in every report, agreeing
  one-to-one (prompt §7; gate check 13). No table in the accepted corpus has them.
- **`extraction_metadata`** carries `extractor` = `Claude, PDF_TO_JSON_PROMPT single-pass`,
  `prompt_version` = `3.8.1`, and `model` as stamped by `tools/stamp_model.py` (gate check 14). The
  accepted corpus has 13 different `extractor` spellings and 17 tables with no prompt version.
- Tables extracted under v3.7.2 were never run with v3.7.3's rule that a table of
  notes-about-activities is converted to annotations rather than typed `reference`. The corpus has
  no `reference` table left, so a new one is a new finding, not an expected delta.

## 3. Row-audit baseline

`soa2usdm-row-audit` compares extracted activity labels against what the pages actually print —
the one completeness check that cannot be built from the JSON alone. Re-run 2026-09-26 on the
accepted corpus:

```
22/22 protocols, 43 tables, 10 on-page-not-extracted, 94 extracted-not-on-page
```

**Acceptance: 10 must not rise.** Any new on-page-not-extracted entry is a dropped row until
proven otherwise. The 10 are listed in the baseline JSON (`row_audit.on_page_not_extracted_items`)
and none is a dropped activity: NCT02107703 T2 header and Protocol Reference column text (4), NCT03283098
T1–T3 footnote fragments (3), a wrapped NCT03817853 label read twice (2), and an NCT01847274 T2 note
line (1). The one schedule page in no extraction is NCT03637764 PDF p.5, out of scope by decision
(§5.1).

**Why the earlier figure was 33 over 45 tables.** 33 was measured on the pre-sweep-1 corpus
(prompt v3.0.2–v3.1.0), which had 45 tables: sweep 1 merged CDISC_Pilot T2 and NCT04004988 T2 into
their T1s (45 → 43) and took the audit from 33 to 10. The pre-sweep-1 baseline file was never
regenerated after that promotion. The 94 extracted-not-on-page is not comparable with the earlier
88 either: the declared-page-window rule (`e8d3940`, 2026-09-02) changed page assignment after
sweep 1.

Blind spots the audit cannot cover, so they need human eyes — 11 tables are assigned no pages: no
text layer on the table pages (NCT02291289 T1–T5, NCT03693430 T1, NCT04557384 T1–T3) and rotated
text (NCT03548935 T1, NCT03548987 T1). NCT04320615 T3, a blind spot before, now gets its pages from
the declared window.

## 4. Corrections disposition — the 29 live corrections, predicted before the sweep

Confirming a prediction is review. Inspecting an unexplained diff is archaeology. Each sidecar is
predicted **retired** (v3.8.x produces it natively), **still needed** (must be re-authored), or
**contingent** (retires only if a specific invariant holds).

10 sidecars exist; 4 have content. The other 6 (NCT02107703 T1, T2; NCT04677179 T1–T4) are empty:
Family A (54 corrections, the NCT02107703 Protocol Reference column) and Family C (3, the
NCT04677179 T4 Dosing row) were retired in sweep 1 because the extraction now produces them
natively. That makes them invariants of the fresh extraction (§7), not corrections.

### 4.1 NCT01847274 T3 · 12 `annotations set` on `annotation_marker` · predicted STILL NEEDED

Human research verdicts on markers 4–15, which Table 9 prints but never defines (doc p.78 defines
only 1–3). Reached by reading §7.4 of the full protocol: 7 hypotheses confirmed against the
Procedures-by-Visit narrative, 3 retained as unconfirmed hypotheses, 1 unresolved (marker 4, no
candidate), 1 flagged as a source-internal contradiction (marker 10, RECIST cadence — Table 7 fn22
says every 6 cycles post-C14, §7.4.2 says every 3).

No re-extraction can reproduce these. The prompt correctly emits the markers with text stating the
definition is not printed; the verdicts live only in the sidecar. They match on
`annotation_marker`, so they re-apply unchanged as long as markers 4–15 keep their names — which
they did in sweep 1.

### 4.2 NCT04557384 T3 · 14 `activities set` on `row_position` · CONTINGENT

Renames bare ordinals `N` to `Sample N`: the source column is headed "Sample #", so a bare ordinal
would publish as an activity called "1". Retires only if the fresh extraction names the rows from
that column itself. Otherwise re-author against the new `row_position`s — the least stable key
there is, on a table with no text layer.

### 4.3 NCT05176314 T1 (2) and NCT05324124 T1 (1) · `activities add` · predicted STILL NEEDED

One `CCI` placeholder row per observed redaction band. The prompt deliberately does not emit
redacted rows, and the number of rows under a band is not determinable (NCT05324124's band is
~141 pt against ~17 pt rows). The rows are kept because `resolve.REDACTED_NAME_PATTERN` keys
`is_redacted` off that placeholder token. Re-derive each `row_position` against the new extraction
before re-applying.

### 4.4 The duplication trap — read this before re-running the pipeline

`corrections.apply_corrections` implements `op: add` as a bare append:

```python
if op == "add":
    arr.append(c["set"])
```

No match key, no dedup. If a fresh extraction emits a CCI row natively **and the old sidecar is
still in place**, ApplyCorrections silently appends a duplicate. Nothing errors.

**Rule: set the 3 `add` ops aside before the pipeline runs over a re-extracted table**, and
re-apply them only after their `row_position` has been re-derived.

Match-key stability, for re-authoring cost:

| Match key | Count | Survives re-extraction? |
|---|---:|---|
| `annotation_marker` | 12 | Yes, if marker names are stable |
| `row_position` | 14 | **No** — positions shift |
| none (`op: add`) | 3 | N/A — appends blindly, see trap above |

### 5.4 The binding gotcha that cost a silent revision

`resolve` attaches an annotation to an activity through the **row's `annotation_markers` string**,
not through `marker_locations`. Correcting `marker_locations` alone leaves the raw JSON looking
right while `referenced_xacts` still shows the old activity, and nothing fails anywhere. v3.6.0 §6
states the rule; a detector for the mismatch now ships in `resolve.py`. **Run it on every
re-extracted table** — this failure is invisible to inspection.

## 6. Page-number convention

Three different page numbers are in play and mixing them has already caused one confusing
exchange. State which is meant, every time:

- **PDF page** — index into the sliced `_soa.pdf`
- **doc page** — index into the full protocol PDF; what extractions and reports use
- **printed footer page** — what a human reads on the page. For NCT04677179 this runs **one lower**
  than the doc page. It is per-protocol and not derivable.

## 7. Per-study criteria

The claims live in `re-extraction-baseline-mined.json`, not in this file. They were mined from the
22 uncertainty reports of the accepted corpus, one agent per report, each reading only its own
report. Three kinds:

- **invariants** — facts about the source a correct extraction of the same PDF must reproduce
  (tables and types, page spans, stated counts, markers, specific marks). Each carries a
  `how_to_check` stated as a test on the extraction JSON.
- **judgement calls** — choices between defensible readings, with the `alternative` the report
  itself names. A fresh extraction that chooses the alternative is a delta to **review**, not a
  regression.
- **source defects** — problems in the protocol itself, with what the extraction did about them.
  They must still be flagged, never repaired (§5.3).

Every `evidence_quote` is a verbatim substring of its report; `tools/verify_mined.py` checks this
mechanically (758 of 758 on 2026-09-26). Every other field paraphrases the report, except
`how_to_check`. The mechanically testable invariants (126: counts, table types, page ranges, file
counts) were run against the accepted corpus and all hold; a sample of the prose checks was
verified by hand.

## 8. Per-table promotion gate

A re-extracted table is promoted into the corpus only when all of these pass. Numbers are the
check ids `tools/gate.py` prints; 8 and 11 run in `tools/dryrun.py`; 15 and 16 are review steps.
Run in order — later checks assume earlier ones held.

In the accepted corpus, checks 13 and 14 report CHECK rather than FAIL because it predates
`review_items` and the provenance fields. **For sweep-2 output, either one at CHECK counts as a
failure**: a v3.8.1 table must carry both.

`gate.py` (per staged study):

1. **Schema valid** against `soa-table-extraction.schema.json`, `schema_version` 1.0,
   `extraction_status` `ready_for_resolution`.
2. **No orphan annotations** — every annotation has ≥1 `marker_locations` entry.
3. **Markers agree both ways** — every marker in an annotation's `marker_locations` also appears in
   that row's `annotation_markers` (§5.4; the `resolve.py` detector, not eyeballing).
4. **No containment pairs** — no annotation's text is strictly contained in another's. CHECK, not
   FAIL: one source-faithful pair exists; verify against the page.
5. **Typing not degenerate** — `by_type` across >20 annotations is not all `source_note`.
   **5a** — a bare cross-reference typed as a footnote is flagged (§6 types these `source_note`).
6. **Page coverage** — every page in `page_start..page_end` contributed activity rows, or the
   report says why not.
7. **Counts vs §2** — the delta table `gate.py` prints for every staged table; every delta
   bucketed (§2.1 / regression / new finding). A baseline table missing from staging FAILS.
9. **Merged marks carry `source_range`.**
10. **`track_label` only on `track` tables** (and a CHECK when it runs to more than 4 words).
12. **Bindings did not vanish** — distinct markers on rows vs the baseline `bind`; FAIL when they
    collapse while the rows are still there.
13. **`review_items` agree one-to-one** with the report's `## Decisions needed (N)` block.
14. **Provenance** — `prompt_version` equals the repo prompt's, and `model` is stamped.
- **Quote fidelity** — every quotation in the report is a verbatim substring of the PDF text or
  the extraction; read each unverified one before trusting the rate.

`dryrun.py` (scratch collection):

8. **Row audit** — on-page-not-extracted must not rise above 10 (§3).
11. **Deterministic layers** — ApplyCorrections → Resolve → Consolidate runs with 0 errors.

Review:

15. **Study criteria** — §7 invariants reproduced or bucketed, §7 source defects still flagged,
    §5 adjudicated findings not re-litigated.
16. **Corrections** — each sidecar retired, re-authored, or confirmed contingent (§4); the `add`
    duplication trap checked (§4.4).

## 9. Rules for the extraction agents

Binding constraints for the Phase 2 fan-out, over and above the extraction prompt:

- **Write only into the staging area.** Never into `collections/*/protocols/*/SoA2USDM/`, and
  never anywhere under `tests/`. Promotion is a separate gated step.
- **The blind covers memory and Project content, not only folders.** Extraction sessions run
  outside the SoA2USDM Project, with memory generation off and only the blind folder connected: a
  memory or Project read carries study answers and leaves no file path. `audit_blinding.py`
  reports any memory, Project or past-chat tool call as LEAK.
- **`tests/fixtures/negative/` is untouchable.** Those five files are deliberately defective
  historical snapshots — the negative controls each detector must fire on. Regenerating them from
  clean output silently deletes the entire negative-control set while the suite still passes green.
- **An honest `unresolved` beats a guess.** Where a marker's target cannot be determined, keep the
  location with the `row_position` where the marker is printed and set
  `location_type: "unresolved"`. A guessed target is a defect that surfaces weeks later.
- **Never repair the source.** Undefined footnotes, redactions and contradictions are data. §5.3
  lists the known ones; a fresh "resolution" of any of them is fabrication.
- **Quote fidelity is mechanical.** Any quotation offered as evidence in the uncertainty report
  must be a verbatim substring of the source. The sweep-1 mining pass produced 2 composed
  quotes out of 422 — plausible, well-formatted, and not in the document. Verify, do not
  trust.
- **Record method, not confidence** (§1e). A method names a re-derivable procedure that can be
  checked against the PDF; a confidence number cannot.

## 10. What this checklist cannot tell you

Stated so the gate is not mistaken for a proof:

- It cannot detect a row that is missing from **both** the baseline and the new extraction. The
  row audit covers this for text-layer pages, but 3 protocols have no text layer at all
  (NCT02291289, NCT03693430, NCT04557384) and 2 are rotated (NCT03548935, NCT03548987).
- It cannot validate a mark that is wrong in the same way in both versions.
